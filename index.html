Autonomous River Cleaning Robot

A robot that collects floating waste from the surface of a river, so cleanup needs less manual effort and fewer people in unsafe water.

Problem statement

Rivers carry floating waste such as plastic bottles, bags and other debris. It harms aquatic life, blocks water flow and lowers water quality. Cleanup today is mostly manual: it is slow, costly, hard to run continuously and unsafe for workers in polluted or fast-moving water.

Who it helps: municipal bodies, river-cleanup groups and the communities that depend on the river.

Our solution

The robot moves across the water surface and collects floating waste into an onboard bin. This reduces human effort and risk, and allows cleaning to continue for longer and at lower cost.

Robot design

The body and part layout were designed so that components fit, stay accessible and suit use on water.
